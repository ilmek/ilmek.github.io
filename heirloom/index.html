Seven styles

One app for every kind of damage

Restore Old Photo

Best for old, damaged or blurry portraits. Repairs faces, fixes scratches and brings back lost detail.

Colorize B&W

Adds realistic, historically accurate colour to a black-and-white photograph.

Enhance Portrait

Sharpens facial detail and improves skin tones on a photo that's already in decent shape.

Clean Up

Removes only scratches, dust and creases. Keeps the original colours and sharpness.

Focus Magic

A general retouch that makes a photo look like a fresh, modern shot.

Fix Blur

For heavily blurred or out-of-focus shots. Rebuilds sharp edges and detail in faces.

Reduce Noise

Removes grain, high-ISO noise and scanning speckle while keeping detail natural.

Batch restore

Add a whole box of photos and restore them in one go. Turn on Auto-save and every result goes straight to Photos.

Animate

Make portraits move

Turn a still portrait into a short video. Pick a motion style and Heirloom does the rest in a few minutes.

  • Come to LifeGentle breathing, soft blinking and a slight head turn.
  • Gentle BreezeHair, leaves and fabric moving in a light breeze.
  • CelebrationSmiles widening, a hint of laughter, warm festive sparkle.
  • CinematicSlow motion with a gentle dolly push or soft pan.
  • Ken BurnsClassic slow zoom and pan. No subject movement.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can my photo be fixed?

Most likely. Scratches, creases, stains and fading go in one pass. If part of a face is missing, the AI rebuilds it: a good guess, not a recovery.

Tips for the best result?

Scan at 600 dpi, or photograph the print flat in daylight without flash. Keep the original; it's the real record.

Can I try it free?

Yes. New accounts get 2 free credits. After that, small one-time packs, no subscription and no watermark.

How long does it take?

About 30 seconds a photo. Animations take 1 to 5 minutes.

What if a photo fails?

You keep your credit. You're only charged for finished results.

Which languages?

The app is in English and Turkish.

Are my photos private?

Yes. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored, where and for how long.
