We only access the photos you select. We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How your photos are processed by AI

When you restore or animate a photo, it is sent to Google's Gemini (images) and Veo (video) models through Google Cloud Vertex AI. Google processes it only to produce your result. Under Google Cloud's terms, your photos are not used to train Google's models. Nothing is sent until you agree in the app, and only the photos you choose are sent.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (EU, Frankfurt): sign-in, database and photo storage.
  • Google Cloud (EU and US): our servers and the Vertex AI models that process photos.
  • RevenueCat (US): verifies in-app purchases.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in and payments. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Expo (US): delivers push notifications.

These providers act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. Some of them process data outside your country, including in the United States, under standard contractual safeguards.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your photos for marketing or to train AI models.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a photo: remove it from History at any time.
  • Delete your account: Account → Delete account. This immediately removes your account, photos, results, history, credits and purchase records. See how to delete your account.
  • Notifications and email tips: turn them off in your device settings or in Account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict processing, and complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to meet legal obligations such as keeping payment records, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure. We send your photos to the AI provider with your consent, which you can withdraw by not uploading further photos and deleting your existing ones.

Children

Heirloom is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos are stored in private storage that only your account can reach, through short-lived links.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
