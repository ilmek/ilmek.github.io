Delete your Heirloom account

In the app

  1. Open Heirloom and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Heirloom's access to your Apple ID.

Without the app

Email mertkarayel94@hotmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Heirloom account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and name
  • All your photos, restorations, animations and history
  • Your credits and purchase records
  • Your notification settings

What is kept

  • A one-way hash of your email address, so the free welcome credits aren't given again to the same address. It can't be turned back into your address.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of your payments under their terms.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically within 30 days.

Deleting your account doesn't refund unused credits. For refunds, contact Apple or Google.
