How it works

From clip to finished video in four steps

1

Pick a video

Choose a clip from your gallery or Files — MP4 or MOV, up to 5 minutes. Keep, lower or remove its original sound.

2

Add your words

Type or paste a script, or record your own voice. The fit meter tells you if it matches the clip before you start.

3

Choose a voice

Pick a voice and a language. Writing in one language and voicing in the other? Tempoframe translates it.

4

Fine-tune and export

Every sentence is a block on the timeline — drag to adjust. Add music and subtitles, then export and share.

Use cases

Made for videos that need a voice

If your video tells a story, sells something or teaches something, a clear voice makes people stay. Here's where Tempoframe fits best.

Real estate & property tours

Turn a walkthrough clip into a listing video with a calm narrator.

Example script“Floor-to-ceiling windows, sea views from every room, and a terrace made for sunsets.”

Recipes & food

Explain each step over your cooking footage — no talking to the camera.

Example script“Brown the onions for two minutes, then add the tomatoes and let it simmer.”

Travel & vlogs

Narrate trips, hidden spots and city guides for Reels and Shorts.

Example script“Hidden between the peaks, this alpine lake is pure calm.”

Product & UGC ads

Add a punchy voiceover to product demos and unboxings.

Example script“Charges in 20 minutes, lasts all day — and it fits in your pocket.”

Explainers & tutorials

Walk viewers through an app, a process or a lesson, step by step.

Example script“First, open settings. Then tap Privacy and turn on the new filter.”

Faceless channels

Run a channel without showing your face or recording your own voice.

Example script“Five facts about the deep ocean you probably didn’t know.”

Two languages, one video

Write once and voice it in English or Turkish to reach a second audience.

Example script“Script in Turkish, voiceover in English — translated for you.”

Use your own voice

Record yourself and use it as-is, or let an AI voice read your words instead.

Example script“Use my recording” or “AI reads it” — your choice.
Features

Everything between “raw clip” and “post”

Natural AI voices

Warm storyteller, bright and energetic, or deep and calm — with speed, pitch and delivery controls on Pro.

Auto-sync

Each sentence is placed where it belongs in your video. Re-align everything with one tap.

Drag to time

Move any sentence block on the timeline, nudge it, speed it up or regenerate it.

Subtitles that pop

Plain, boxed or accent styles, at the bottom or middle, burned right into the video.

Music that steps aside

Royalty-free tracks that duck under the voice. Keep, lower or remove the original audio.

Translate & fit

Translate your script and shorten it automatically when it’s longer than the clip.

Export anywhere

9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 at 720p, 1080p or 4K. Save to Photos or share in a tap.

HDR in, HDR out

HDR footage stays HDR, so your colors don’t go flat.

FAQ
Which languages are supported?

Voiceovers are available in English and Turkish, and Tempoframe can translate your script between them. The app itself is in English and Turkish.

Do I need to record my voice?

No. Type or paste a script and an AI voice reads it. If you prefer, you can record yourself and use the recording as-is or have the AI read your words.

How long can my video be?

Up to 5 minutes, MP4 or MOV, from your gallery or Files.

Can I use the music commercially?

The built-in tracks are royalty-free (CC0).

What happens to my videos?

Your content is processed only to create your voiceover. See the Privacy Policy for details.
