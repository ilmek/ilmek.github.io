Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my subscription?
Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel Tempoframe Pro?
Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tempoframe. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. It is deleted within 30 days and we email you when it is done.

Why can't I save my video to Photos?
Allow Tempoframe to add photos in Settings → Tempoframe → Photos.
